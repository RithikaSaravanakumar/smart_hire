✨ Core Features

Engineered for High-Velocity Placement

Everything candidates and talent acquisition teams need to evaluate competency, schedule interviews, and finalize offers without friction.

Skill-Fit Analytics

Direct Recruiter Channels

How SmartHire Works

A clear, four-step path designed to guide students from account setup to a confirmed placement offer.

01 Account

Create Profile

Register your student profile, upload your resume, and catalog your verified technical skill stack.

02 Match

Skill Fit Discovery

The system evaluates openings against your competencies and highlights your matching skills and prerequisites.

03 Apply

One-Click Submission

Submit applications directly to active campus placement drives and track interview status in real time.

04 Placement

Hiring & Selection

Complete selection assessments, connect directly with corporate recruiters, and receive placement confirmation.
